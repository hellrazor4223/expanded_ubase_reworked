ABOUT THIS MOD:

This mod changes the interior of Alien Bases into a tactical maze. The tactical maze is generated from 100 map modules.
Included are N, S, W, E turned versions of all maptiles, also for vanilla ones.

New 20x20 sized facilities have been added:
 - Clonelabs
 - Scout Ship Docking Bays
 - Food Processing
 - Alternate Large Storage

Also a lot of small 10x10 sized map modules have been added for more variety as well.
You can and will explore a bigger variety of alien gardens, small bunkers, corridors, some with good ambush points.

For the Final Mission a Fake Alien Brain Modules has been added, and the vanilla Alien Brain modules have received turned Versions also.

This mod increases the mapsize for Alien Base Missions to 60x60 and Final game mission to 80x80.
Also i am using shade setting 10. Still night for your soldiers but you can actually see something.

History:
luke83 original work inspired me to rework and bugfix his original maps.
After that the collection of maps grew over time and now including the vanilla UBASE maps the tactical maze has been finished.


If you have any feedback for me, want suggest our own maptiles or ideas added, please let me know.
Easiest way is to post in the forum here: https://openxcom.org/forum/index.php/topic,3580.0.html
